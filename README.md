### Measures of central tendency
#### Mean
* **Statistical notation:** $\bar{X} = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} X_i$
* **R code:** `mean(x = afl_margins)`
*  **Note:** Calculate the arithmetic average of a numeric variable.
